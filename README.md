## What Do You Need for IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV?

Samsung Smart TVs use the Tizen platform, and Samsung's own support documentation explains that apps are installed through the TV's App Store. Samsung also notes that app availability can depend on the TV model and software.

That matters because an IPTV subscription and an IPTV player are two different things.

The subscription provides the streaming credentials. The player is the app that actually loads the channels on your television.

TiviLive's current Samsung setup guide lists **Smarters Player Lite, IBO Player and Smart IPTV** as options. After installing a compatible player, the guide says to enter the username, password and server URL supplied in the welcome email, or use an M3U URL/Xtream Codes login where supported.

Samsung's own TV app listings also show several third-party IPTV players that support formats such as M3U/M3U8 and Xtream-style credentials. Those apps generally act as players rather than providing channels themselves.

## How to Set Up a Samsung Smart TV IPTV Subscription

The process is fairly straightforward.

First, connect the Samsung TV to the internet and open **Apps** from the Smart Hub. Samsung's current instructions say to search for the desired app and install it directly from the App Store.

Next, install a compatible IPTV player. TiviLive specifically lists Smarters Player Lite, IBO Player and Smart IPTV in its Samsung installation guide.

Then open the player and choose the login method it supports. For TiviLive, that can mean entering the Xtream Codes API information or the M3U URL from the welcome email.

Finally, let the EPG load. TiviLive says the electronic programme guide may take a few minutes to populate during the first setup.

And there is one small Samsung-specific catch: an app that works perfectly on one TV may not appear on another. Samsung says only apps available in the TV's App Store can be installed, and compatibility can vary by model.
